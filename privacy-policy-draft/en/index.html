PokeAndScan Privacy Policy

Published by FalconSocka. PokeAndScan is an independent Android companion. It is not an official Pokémon GO or Niantic service and is not affiliated with or endorsed by them.

Last updated: 23 September 2026

English Slovenčina

This page describes the current app and the planned MVP scanning features. The current app build is an early scaffold: it stores your language, appearance, and introduction-completion preferences on your device. Scanning, review, and export are not available in this build. The sections below describe how those features are designed to work when they are released.

On-device data and processing

The planned MVP processes user-selected game-screen content on your Android device. Recognition is local and offline. PokeAndScan does not require an account or a PokeAndScan backend, does not use cloud OCR, and does not upload captured screens. The current build has no analytics, advertising, or tracking code, and the planned MVP has no third-party data-collection service.

When scanning is available, the app will handle scan and snapshot metadata; recognized or manually entered Pokémon fields; review and correction history; and evidence crops needed to review results. Clean records keep a minimal representative appraisal crop. Uncertain, partial, corrected, or nickname-related fields may keep relevant field crops. A missed appraisal event may keep a transition crop. Non-game frames are classified in memory and discarded; they are not retained as images. The full source recording is not copied or retained by default.

Screen capture and selected recordings

Live capture will start only after you choose it and grant Android's screen-capture consent. PokeAndScan does not control Pokémon GO, tap or swipe for you, or capture microphone or system audio. On Android versions that require it, notification permission is requested when you start a live scan, not during setup. A foreground-service notification is used for background processing; notification permission is not requested solely for MP4 processing.

For an MP4 scan, you choose a recording using Android's file picker or share flow. The app processes its content URI without copying the whole video by default and temporarily keeps access only while processing or while an unfinished scan may need recovery. Audio tracks are ignored. Releasing access does not delete or modify your original recording.

Storage, backup, and deletion

App preferences are stored on the device. The planned MVP keeps snapshots, review data, and evidence in app-managed local storage. Deleting a snapshot removes its app-owned records, review data, and evidence, but does not delete a source MP4 stored outside the app. The app does not provide a delete-all-data command; to clear all app data, use Android Settings.

Android Auto Backup is disabled for PokeAndScan. Backup rules also exclude app data from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Some Android manufacturers may not honor device-to-device transfer exclusions.

Exports

When available, CSV and JSON exports will be created only when you ask for them. You can save or share an export outside app-managed storage. PokeAndScan cannot delete or control copies saved or shared elsewhere. These exports are not a full backup of snapshots, evidence, or review history.
